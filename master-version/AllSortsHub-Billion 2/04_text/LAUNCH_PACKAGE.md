## 3. Five Thumbnail Concepts

1. **The Deadpan Stare.** Extreme close-up of Ravi's dead-eyed face from Scene 1, phone held up beside his cheek, screen blown out bright with the ten-figure number. Deep black vignette, one hard rim light. Sells the joke without explaining it.
2. **Golden Toilet Throne.** Ravi lounging on the gold toilet in sunglasses holding a burger, chandelier spotlight, cash raining down. Absurd, instantly readable at 120px wide, zero context needed.
3. **Split Panel — Before / After.** Hard vertical split: left, Ravi mid-air cheering in front of the mansion; right, Ravi hollow-eyed on the curb at dusk. Big red downward arrow across the seam.
4. **The Scream.** The fisheye Scene 8 shot — mouth impossibly wide, eyes bulging white, phone glowing in frame, radial blue shock lines. Highest raw-emotion option.
5. **Ravi vs. Max.** Ravi on the left with sparkling greedy eyes clutching a fan of cash; Max on the right in tight close-up, flat unimpressed stare, glasses glinting. Explosive gold rays behind. Sells the duo dynamic — best option for building a recurring series.

## 5. Five Shorts, With Titles and Opening Hooks

### Short 1 — "The Notification"
**Source:** Scene 1 (shots 01–02)
**Hook (0–2s):** Phone buzzes. Screen fills the frame: *$1,000,000,000 DEPOSITED*. Cut to Ravi's completely dead expression.
**Payoff:** "Okay... either I'm a billionaire... or I've finally broken the banking system."

### Short 2 — "Do NOT Spend It"
**Source:** Scene 2 (shots 05–06)
**Hook (0–2s):** Max's glasses flash white. "Do NOT spend it." Ravi, instantly: "Obviously."
**Payoff:** Hard cut — Ravi is already sitting in the hypercar. "I'm just looking!"

### Short 3 — "One Of Everything"
**Source:** Scene 3 (shots 07–08)
**Hook (0–2s):** "One of everything." Then a rapid-fire cut sequence: mansion, pizza mountain, jet, 500 burgers.
**Payoff:** Lands on the solid gold toilet under a chandelier. "Obviously the golden toilet."

### Short 4 — "Change The World"
**Source:** Scene 4 (shots 09–10)
**Hook (0–2s):** Reporters swarming, flashbulbs. "What are you going to do with a billion dollars?"
**Payoff:** "Change the world." Cut to Ravi riding a giant inflatable dinosaur in a pool. Max stares into the camera.

### Short 5 — "What Did You Learn?"
**Source:** Scene 8 (shots 15–16)
**Hook (0–2s):** Two guys on a curb at dusk. Phone reads *BALANCE: $47.32*. "So... what did you learn?"
**Payoff:** "Never trust free money." *ping* — *$47.32 REMOVED* — "WAIT. WHAT?!"

> Vertical reframe note: every one of these five is built on a shot with the character in the centre third, so a 9:16 centre crop holds without losing the gag.

## 7. Production Notes

**Status of this package.** The 17 storyboard keyframes, the full dialogue track, the three music cues and the ten sound effects are all produced and included. The animated clips were **not** rendered — `video_generate` is gated behind a paid Runable plan and every render attempt failed. Enable it and the 17 frames double as the exact start frames for the 17 clips.

**Character lock.** Ravi: messy black hair, red-orange zip hoodie over white tee, dark grey joggers, chunky white sneakers. Max: neat dark brown hair, round black glasses, teal-blue button-up over grey tee, navy jeans, brown loafers. The two model sheets are the reference of record — every frame was generated against them.

**Voice cast.** Ravi = `chris` (young, energetic, immature). Max = `george` (calm, dry, increasingly done with this). Reporter = `sarah`. Mansion owner = `charlie`.

**Audio assembly.** Dialogue, score and SFX are delivered as separate stems on purpose — mix them on a timeline in an editor, not by concatenating files. Cue map: comedy score under shots 01–10, noir cue under shots 11–13, sad piano under shots 15–17 with the slide whistle landing on the final gag.

**Legal.** Original characters, original story, original generated music and SFX. No existing franchise characters, no licensed music, no third-party footage.
